Kutty Buddy - Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Kutty Buddy is a child-friendly educational app designed to provide interactive learning through games, tracing activities, rhymes, animals, numbers, colours, shapes and a friendly 3D Buddy character.

Information We Collect

Kutty Buddy does not require users to create an account and the core learning features are designed to work offline.

The app does not intentionally collect personal information such as a child's name, email address, phone number, home address or other personal details for remote storage.

How Information Is Used

The app uses information entered during normal app use, such as a child's chosen name, only to provide the requested in-app experience. Such information is used locally within the app and is not intentionally sent to a remote server by the core app features.

Audio and Voice

Kutty Buddy may use the device's text-to-speech functionality to provide spoken learning content. Voice generation is handled by the device's available speech service.

Audio, Rhymes and Animal Sounds

The app includes bundled learning audio such as rhymes and animal sounds. These files are included locally in the application and are intended to work without requiring an internet connection.

Children's Privacy

Kutty Buddy is intended for children and educational use. The app is designed not to knowingly collect personal information from children through its core offline learning features.

Third-Party Services

The current core app experience does not require users to create accounts or submit personal information to use the learning activities.

Data Security

Because the core learning features are designed for offline use and do not require a user account, there is no need for users to transmit personal information to use those features.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app or its privacy practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page.
